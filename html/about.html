About Cafe-Creme

A neighbourhood cafe inspired by Indian hospitality, slow mornings, and the simple pleasure of a well-made cup.

Made to feel familiar

We started Cafe-Creme with one idea: coffee tastes better when there is time to enjoy it. Our menu brings together comforting Indian favourites, carefully sourced coffee, and bakes made in small batches.

Come in for a quick chai, stay for a conversation, or make our tables your own for the afternoon.

“Every cup should feel like a warm welcome.”
